Legal · Privacy

Your Data Rights

Last updated: May 2025 · Applies to People Memory v1.1.0 and later

Because People Memory stores all data exclusively on your device, you already have complete, immediate control over every piece of information at all times. You don't need to submit a request to us — your data is yours, always.
👁️

Right of Access

View all data stored about the people in your life at any time, within the app.

→ Open any person's profile in the app.

✏️

Right to Rectification

Correct or update any inaccurate data immediately, without waiting for us.

→ Tap Edit on any person's profile.

🗑️

Right to Erasure

Delete any individual contact or wipe all data completely at any time.

→ Settings → Clear All Data, or delete per-person.

📦

Right to Data Portability

Export your data as a JSON file you can keep, move, or import elsewhere.

→ Settings → Export Data.

🚫

Right to Object

Object to any specific processing of your data. Since we process nothing on our servers, there is nothing to object to.

→ No action needed.

🔒

Right to Restrict Processing

Restrict how your data is used. All processing happens on-device and only when you directly interact with the app.

→ Already restricted by design.

🌍 Who This Applies To

These rights apply to all users of People Memory worldwide. Users in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, and California (CCPA) have these rights guaranteed by law. Because of our offline-first, no-server architecture, we meet — and in most cases exceed — the requirements of GDPR, UK GDPR, and CCPA by design.

Our privacy model means you never need to wait days for a data access or deletion request to be fulfilled. Everything is instant and in your hands.

📲 How to Exercise Your Rights In-App

All rights can be exercised directly inside People Memory:

  • 1 Access your data — tap any person's card to view all stored information including notes, trust level, interaction history, and dates.
  • 2 Edit or correct data — tap the edit icon on any profile to update any field instantly.
  • 3 Delete a single person — open their profile, scroll to the bottom, and tap Delete Contact. This is permanent and immediate.
  • 4 Delete all data — go to Settings → Clear All Data. This wipes every contact, note, photo, and voice note from your device.
  • 5 Export your data — go to Settings → Export Data to download a JSON backup you can store anywhere.

🏛️ GDPR & CCPA Notes

Under GDPR (EU) and UK GDPR, you have rights including access, rectification, erasure, portability, restriction, and objection. Under CCPA (California), you have rights to know, delete, and opt out of sale. People Memory satisfies all of these through its offline architecture — we have no personal data on our servers to access, modify, or sell.

We do not sell, share, or broker personal data. We have no user accounts, no tracking, no analytics, and no third-party SDKs. There is nothing to opt out of.

Legal basis for processing (GDPR Article 6): All data processing occurs locally on your device based on your explicit action (Article 6(1)(a) — consent, and Article 6(1)(b) — performance of a service you requested). No processing occurs without your direct input.
